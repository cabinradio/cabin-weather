Cabin Weather

Weather for the Northwest Territories, from Cabin Radio. The page itself is still being built; the data feed is live:

  • data/current.json – current conditions for 28 communities, refreshed every 30 minutes
  • data/forecast/{community}.json – next 24 hours and seven days
  • data/history/{community}.json – recent weather against the record, for six communities

Based on Environment and Climate Change Canada data.
